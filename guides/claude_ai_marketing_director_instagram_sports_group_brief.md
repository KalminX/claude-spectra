## 4. Scope of Work: The 7 Core Marketing Pillars

### 1. Multi-Brand Knowledge Architecture
- **Knowledge Isolation & Organization:** Create dedicated Claude Project spaces or unified structured markdown repositories for each of the 5 brands.
- **Brand Dossiers:** Standardizing brand voice, visual aesthetics (hex colors, fonts, logo usage), audience demographics (parents, youth players, amateur athletes), historical top-performing content, and custom AI system instructions.
- **Admin Maintenance:** Designing simple markdown/text templates so internal staff can add new campaigns or update brand rules without technical assistance.

### 2. Instagram & Meta Performance Intelligence
- **Data Integration:** Connecting Meta Graph API / export data into Claude to ingest weekly metrics (views, saves, shares, retention rates, reach).
- **Weekly Automated Digest:** Generating an executive performance breakdown answering:
  - *What worked best this week and why?*
  - *Which posts underperformed?*
  - *Actionable recommendations and fixes for next week.*

### 3. AI Content Strategist & Videographer Shot-Lists
- **Dynamic Content Planning:** Allows the marketing manager to ask: *"What should Wolves Academy post this week?"* and receive a complete 7-day multi-format plan (Reels, Carousels, Stories).
- **Production Directives for Videographers:** Translating strategic ideas into actionable filming briefs (camera angles, audio hooks, b-roll required from football training sessions).

### 4. Sports Trends & Competitor Monitoring
- **Trend Identification Framework:** Prompt routines analyzing emerging Instagram formats, trending audio concepts, viral football challenges, and local GCC competitor strategies to adapt them authentically.

### 5. Creative Copywriting & Design Integration
- **Full Creative Packages:** Generating viral hook variations, slide-by-slide carousel copy, caption variations, and hashtag clusters.
- **Design Tool Connection:** Integrating Canva MCP or AI visual generation to output first-draft visual layouts and template suggestions for human designers to polish.

### 6. Content Scheduling & Approval Workflow
- **Frictionless Publishing Loop:** `Claude Plan` $\rightarrow$ `Creative Asset Created` $\rightarrow$ `Marketing Team One-Click Review` $\rightarrow$ `Scheduled via Meta Business Suite / Metricool`.

### 7. Meta Ads Advisory (Human-in-the-Loop)
- **Ad Performance Auditing:** Evaluating ad campaign metrics (CPA, CTR, CPM, frequency) to spot creative fatigue, suggest winning creative variations to boost, and recommend budget reallocations across ad sets (with zero automated spend changes).
