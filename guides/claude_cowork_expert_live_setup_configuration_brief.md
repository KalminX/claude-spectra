## 3. Core Scope of Configuration & Functional Areas

### A. Multi-Company Workspace & Project Architecture
- **Global vs. Project Instructions:** Structuring master global behavioral rules alongside dedicated, context-isolated Project workspaces for each distinct company.
- **Knowledge Base Engineering:** Uploading, organizing, and structuring corporate knowledge (brand guidelines, service offerings, proposal templates, pricing matrices, customer personas) to ensure zero context bleeding between companies.

### B. Claude Cowork, Skills & Tool Connectors
- **Claude Skills Development:** Authoring custom Skills with explicit input schemas, step-by-step reasoning instructions, and standardized output formats for recurring business tasks.
- **Plugins, Connectors & MCP (Model Context Protocol):** Configuring native and custom MCP connectors to link Claude directly to the client's operational stack:
  - **Email & Calendar:** Microsoft 365 / Outlook for automated inbox triaging, draft generation, and meeting prep.
  - **CRM & Marketing Automation:** GoHighLevel (GHL) for pipeline updates, lead categorization, and automated client communication.
  - **Presentation & Documentation:** Gamma AI (slide deck creation), Adobe Acrobat / PDF parsing, and Make.com / Zapier webhooks.

### C. Autonomous Workflows & Scheduled Tasks
- **Scheduled Tasks & Proactive Triggers:** Setting up automated background tasks (e.g., daily morning briefing, pipeline review, unread email triage, weekly KPI reporting).
- **Human-in-the-Loop & Approval Rules:** Establishing clear guardrails where Claude drafts content/emails/proposals autonomously but halts for executive approval on high-stakes actions.
