### B. Filming & Technical Specifications
- **Video Capture:** 4K / 1080p 60fps crystal-clear desktop screen capture paired with high-definition camera talking-head segments.
- **Audio Standards:** Professional studio microphone (Shure SM7B / Rode NT1 / equivalent), noise floor < -60dB, dynamic compression, broadcast loudness normalized to -14 LUFS.
- **Presentation Delivery:** Articulate, engaging, and professional English delivery.

### C. Post-Production & Editing
- **Dynamic Visual Pacing:** Smooth zoom-ins on code snippets and UI elements, lower-third titles, motion-graphics callout cards, cursor highlighting, and background audio beds.
- **Delivery Formats:** Exported high-bitrate MP4 master files, SRT subtitle files, timestamps/chapter markers, and lesson summary PDF cheat-sheets.
